# Style

## Format
- Vertical 9:16 (1080x1920) for Reels, TikTok, Shorts.

## Language
- Keep my words exactly as spoken, including any language mixing; never translate.

## Captions
- Phrase by phrase, 3–6 words, max 2 lines.
- White bold text with a soft shadow, key words in my brand color.
- Upper third, never over my face.

## Hook
- No hook text.

## Pace
- Natural: keep short pauses, cut silences longer than ~1 s.

## Graphics
- Minimal. Only when it helps show what I am saying.

## Color
- Natural, no grade.

## Sound
- Voice only.
