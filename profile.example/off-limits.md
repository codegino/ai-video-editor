# Off-limits

- Politics: parties, elections, politicians, government, policy opinions.
- Gender-related topics: gender identity debates, gender roles, sexism or jokes about any gender.
- Politically incorrect content: stereotypes, slurs, or jokes about race, ethnicity, religion, disability, or other groups.
- Disability: jokes, mockery, or offhand remarks about physical, mental, or learning disabilities.
- Religion: opinions on, criticism of, or jokes about any faith or belief.
- Sexuality: sexual orientation debates, sexual content, or jokes about it.
- Race and ethnicity: comments, comparisons, or jokes about any race, ethnicity, or nationality.
- Mental health and self-harm: suicide, self-harm, or making light of mental illness.
- Tragedies and violence: disasters, deaths, crimes, wars, or terrorism, especially jokes about them.
- Body shaming: comments on weight, looks, or appearance of anyone.
